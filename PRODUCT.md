# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js + Tailwind, static export, deploy on Vercel. Chosen by the user (their existing Vercel MCP is connected). One-page site with anchor navigation.

## Users
Homeowners and small business owners in Tianguá, Ceará (and the wider Serra da Ibiapaba region) who are planning a build, a renovation, or an interior fit-out, and who are looking for a local architect. They arrive mostly from Google Maps / Instagram on a phone, often outside business hours, and their job is: decide in under a minute whether this architect is credible and worth a phone call.

## Product Purpose
A one-page portfolio that presents architect Antonio Rodrigo's work and services, and converts a mobile visitor into a WhatsApp conversation. Success = the visitor taps the phone/WhatsApp CTA.

## Positioning
Local, reachable, and demonstrated: an architect in Tianguá whose own rendered projects (residences, decks, suites, kitchens, dental and hospitality interiors) are shown as the proof, with a direct line to him rather than a contact form. The incumbent site is a free WordPress subdomain with unoptimized full-res PNGs; this replaces it with a fast, self-hosted one-pager.

## Operating Context
- Business runs by phone/WhatsApp; the phone number is the conversion channel, not email.
- Working hours: Mon–Thu 07:00–18:00, Fri 07:00–17:30, Sat–Sun closed. Visitors commonly browse outside those hours.
- Social presence is Instagram `@arquitetoantoniorodrigo`.
- Google Business Profile carries a 5★ rating from 4 reviews — the only public social proof that exists.
- Project photos already exist as architectural renders (they are visualizations, not site photographs).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single page: hero, about, services, project gallery, proof, contact/CTA.
- Anchor navigation; sticky WhatsApp CTA on mobile.
- All imagery must be served locally from the project (no WordPress hotlinks), resized and converted to modern formats.
- Portuguese (pt-BR) is the only language.
- No contact form / no backend: CTAs open `wa.me` and `tel:` links.
- Confirmed facts: phone +55 88 99737-4527 (WhatsApp), address R. Doze de Agosto — Centro, Tianguá - CE, 62320-097, Instagram `@arquitetoantoniorodrigo`.
